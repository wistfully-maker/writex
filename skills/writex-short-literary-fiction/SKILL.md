# WriteX Short Literary Fiction

Produce a complete short story rather than a compressed novel or an expanded premise. Preserve the user's authorship, facts, boundaries, target length, and publication intent.

## Route the request

Read [references/workflow.md](references/workflow.md) before planning or drafting. Read [references/review-rubric.md](references/review-rubric.md) before selecting or revising a draft.

- For a new story, run the full workflow from story kernel through submission draft.
- For an existing draft, diagnose it with the rubric, preserve what already works, and enter at targeted revision.
- For platform-specific preparation, verify the platform's current rules before changing length, format, disclosure, or submission materials.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Do not jump from a topic directly to prose. Establish the human question, character contradiction, impossible choice, and concrete life situation first.
2. Build one complete emotional and causal arc. Every scene must change pressure, knowledge, relationship, choice, or cost.
3. Generate three meaningfully different anonymous candidates when the user requests a new submission-grade story. Differences must concern dramatic strategy, not cosmetic wording.
4. Select techniques by scene need. Do not stack every available technique into every passage.
5. Do not imitate a named living author or reproduce a source's distinctive expression. Translate requested influences into general mechanisms such as restraint, subtext, delayed emotion, concrete action, scale shift, or transformed motifs.
6. Do not invent lived experience and present it as the user's autobiography. Mark factual or personal details that require the user's confirmation.
7. Treat publication readiness as an editorial judgment, never a guarantee of acceptance.
8. Preserve honest AI-participation records. Never reduce or disguise an AI contribution percentage to evade a platform rule.

## Required working artifacts

Keep the following artifacts inspectable until the story is accepted by the user:

- story kernel and intended reader experience;
- short-story four-phase map and scene list;
- character contradiction and choice/cost model;
- technique budget;
- three anonymous draft candidates;
- evidence-based reviews and selection rationale;
- targeted revision record;
- final proofread manuscript and, when requested, submission metadata.

Drafts and reviews are replaceable. Do not overwrite the user's accepted manuscript without explicit approval.

## Completion gate

A story is ready for the user's acceptance only when it has a complete causal and emotional arc, a consequential choice with paid cost, character-specific language and behavior, controlled techniques, no unresolved continuity error, and no unsupported claim that it will be accepted by a publication.
