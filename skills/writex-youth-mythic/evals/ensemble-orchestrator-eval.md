# Stage 7G + Stage 8 Integration Eval

## 7G acceptance test

A complete project should be able to answer:

### Tier A/B
- What do they want outside the protagonist?
- Who matters to them besides the protagonist?
- What changes while they are offscreen?
- What choice do they make that the protagonist did not cause?
- What happens to their own life by the ending?

### Tier C
- What were they doing before the volume plot hit them?
- What do they independently choose?
- What happens to them after the volume ends?

If the answer is missing for a recurring important character, the world risks NPC behavior.

## Example: 《灾厄回收站》

### 魏崇
Can support a Tier B life arc:
- wants a day shift to spend time with daughter;
- has work friendships beyond Chen Du;
- can change shift / family routine offscreen;
- survives to become part of the distributed witness system;
- his ordinary job becomes plot-relevant without making him secretly special.

PASS direction.

### 许川 (Volume 1 future-death target)
Should be Tier C:
- courier work;
- debt / family choice;
- promise to daughter;
- reacts to predicted death in his own way;
- after survival, must have post-volume life consequence.

This prevents him from being "the person the protagonist saves".

## Stage 8 acceptance test

The project should be able to generate from one canon:
- title;
- synopsis;
- editor pitch;
- tags;
- cover brief;
- character visual brief;
- volume poster brief.

All should agree on:
- genre;
- protagonist ability;
- cost;
- relationship model;
- spoiler boundary;
- visual symbols.

No downstream artifact may create a new plot fact.

## Verdict

Architecture:
PASS if all project states and gates are respected.

Final prose quality:
still requires chapter-level human review.
