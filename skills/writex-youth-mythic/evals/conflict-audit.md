# 7A–7F Conflict & Redundancy Audit

## Resolved overlaps

### Normality Reservoir vs Emotional Deposit
Resolution:
- Reservoir = planned inventory at volume/sequence scale.
- Deposit = an actual event referenced by an emotional debt.
No duplicate lists.

### Afterimage Ending vs Afterimage Release vs Residual Image Ending
Resolution:
- one canonical CarrierRegistry;
- structural layer controls where it appears;
- emotional layer controls what debt it pays;
- prose layer controls how quietly it is rendered.

### Relationship Debt vs Emotional Debt
Resolution:
- relationship debt is dyadic state;
- emotional debt is intrapsychic state;
- cross-link IDs allowed.

### Reader Question vs Mystery
Resolution:
- ReaderQuestion is the superset.
- Mystery adds Reveal Ladder fields.

### Loss Shadow
Resolution:
- one LossTarget shared by Character Magnetism and Emotional Orchestration.

### Narrative Technique vs Prose Mode
Resolution:
- technique = narrative purpose/control;
- prose mode = language execution.
A visual-scene technique does not require mythic-battle prose; it may use competence or quiet-emotional mode.

## Tension / contradictions resolved

### "Use humor" vs "protect serious emotion"
Rule:
Humor is character defense and pressure valve, not mandatory beat frequency.
During final impact/release, humor yields unless established psychology requires a small defensive trace.

### "Afterimage endings are strong" vs "ending repetition is bad"
Rule:
Afterimage is optional.
StyleHistory warns on repeated ending forms.
Carrier reuse requires meaning transformation.

### "Mystery keeps pages turning" vs "local closure required"
Rule:
Payoff then widen.
New large question only after a local answer or irreversible narrowing.

### "Power payoff is fun" vs "power cannot solve private deficit"
Rule:
Power can solve external scene problems.
It must not automatically solve the character's core emotional deficit.

### "Narrative richness" vs "prompt overload"
Rule:
Progressive loading and 2-module foreground budget.
Most scenes should not execute all six enhancement layers.

## What remains agent judgment

These cannot be safely hard-coded:
- whether ordinary-life deposits are emotionally sufficient;
- whether a metaphor is fresh and POV-owned;
- whether a character refresh truly adds understanding;
- whether a reveal feels fair;
- whether a scene is moving at the right emotional speed;
- whether a sentence rhythm feels natural;
- whether a relationship asymmetry is compelling rather than contrived.

The system should demand evidence and critique, not pretend these are deterministic facts.


## 7B Character Magnetism vs 7G Character Life
Resolution:
- Magnetism controls reader attachment.
- Life Engine controls independent existence.
A character can be magnetic but still feel like a protagonist satellite; both must be audited.

## Ensemble richness vs narrative focus
Resolution:
- Life details live in ledgers first.
- Only details that alter characterization, choice, stakes, relationship, or future re-entry enter prose.
- Do not create filler subplots merely to prove independence.

## Story canon vs marketing/visual optimization
Resolution:
- Canon always wins.
- Marketing may compress, not fabricate.
- Visual skills may stylize, not rewrite character/world canon.
