# Continuity and Ledgers

After every accepted chapter, extract observations and update ledgers before the next chapter is planned.

Maintain:

- character arc ledger;
- knowledge ledger;
- relationship ledger;
- secret ledger with protection/reveal windows;
- promise ledger;
- foreshadowing ledger;
- motif ledger;
- emotional-debt ledger;
- chapter-pattern signatures;
- supporting-character agency stats.

## Hard rules

- POV context receives only facts the POV character legally knows.
- Secret leakage is veto-level when it violates a protected reveal window.
- Major relationship transitions require bridge evidence.
- Core character change requires trigger evidence.
- High-pressure emotional debt may be touched or escalated before payoff, but may not disappear without a release event.
- Repeating the same ending shape/emotional movement/technique across chapters is a structural risk.
- A recurring supporting character needs visible goals and consequential choices of their own.
