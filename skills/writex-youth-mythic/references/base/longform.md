# Long-form Structure

## Nested four-phase arcs

The book has its own opening -> development -> climax -> ending. Every volume independently has its own opening -> development -> climax -> ending.

A chapter therefore carries two coordinates. Example: `book=development`, `volume=climax`.

Volume climax must create a local irreversible turn and volume ending must close the local promise. If the book is not yet at its allowed payoff phase, protected book promises remain unresolved.

## Rolling horizon

Default planning resolution:

- whole book: stable four-phase arc, global promises, ending cost, volume blueprints;
- every volume: local four-phase arc and local promise;
- near ~8 chapters: sequence-level direction;
- next 2 chapters: detailed chapter directives;
- next scene: detailed only after entering the single-chapter workflow.

## Replanning

Continuity risk, long-range audit, emergent characters, pacing drift, or explicit author change may create a replan proposal. Preserve accepted canon and already-paid promises. Replan proposals always require human approval.
