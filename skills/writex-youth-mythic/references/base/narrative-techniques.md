# Narrative Technique Pack

Techniques are mechanisms, not decoration. Pick only what the chapter's emotional release requires.

## Retrospective narration
Use when later knowledge changes the meaning of an earlier moment. Preserve a real gap between what the present character knows and what the later narrator understands. Avoid habitual "later he would know" phrasing.

## Emotional delay
Separate trigger from interpretation. Let the character continue a concrete action, forbid premature emotional explanation, and release through a later carrier such as an object, gesture, routine, place, or ordinary interruption.

## Character-specific metaphor
Source metaphor domains from the character's lived experience. A genetic engineer may naturally think in cell culture, sequence alignment, contamination, inheritance, or lab routines; this still needs restraint. Generic celestial/oceanic/poetic domains should not leak in unless earned by character history.

## Visual scene
Use space, distance, hands, objects, exits, eye lines, and physical decisions to carry relation change. Avoid screenplay/camera vocabulary.

## Subtext
Make spoken goal and hidden goal diverge under pressure. Do not make every line cryptic. Silence is meaningful only when the reader can infer what the character is protecting.

## Macro / micro scale shift
Move from an abstract or civilizational concern back into a concrete trivial carrier. The small thing must sharpen the large thing, not merely prettify it.

## Motif recall
A recurring motif must transform meaning. Reappearance without changed meaning is stagnation, not payoff.

## Budget
Default: <=4 active techniques per chapter, <=2 primary techniques per scene, <=1 dominant technique signal per beat.
