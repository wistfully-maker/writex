# Research Provenance

The enhancement architecture was developed by mechanism-level analysis of the user's uploaded old-edition *Dragon Raja* corpus across volume structure, character attraction, relationship dynamics, mystery/type pleasure, emotional release, and prose/voice behavior.

The package intentionally contains:
- generalized contracts;
- pattern names;
- state machines;
- evaluation logic;
- source-agnostic prose modes.

It intentionally does **not** contain:
- copied passages;
- scene replicas;
- source-specific characters or world rules as generation templates;
- a named-author style instruction.

The goal is original fiction with similar *functional strengths* (reader drive, attachment, delayed emotion, dynamic voice), not surface imitation.
